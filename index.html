Letsrunfaraway

Letsrunfaraway is the pursuit of a life lived intentionally. Not to escape, but to confront.

Founded by Aditya Shenoy, it is a body of work shaped by raw moments documented through a lens and the decision to build a life worth living, and remembering.

This project explores both the beauty of the world we walk upon and what is uncovered within yourself when you move beyond the familiar, where the lines of the map truly begin to fade, and you choose to willingly step into the unknown.

Only to find out on the other side of that is where,

Adventure begins.

Aditya Shenoy

“I find traces of home not in the arrival, but in the act of moving forward.”
